    ABOUT

    Jintong Yang (杨谨侗) is a multidisciplinary media artist and researcher whose work sits between HCI, time-based media art, and media theory. Informed by phenomenology and critical approaches to technology, she examines how computational systems mediate embodied experience, perception, and expression through artistic practice. Working across animation, performance, and installation, she bridges digital and physical processes in interactive and immersive works. She holds an MFA in Computer Arts from the School of Visual Arts, New York, and is currently pursuing a PhD in Media Arts and Technology at the University of California, Santa Barbara.
